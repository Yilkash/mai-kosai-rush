# AGENTS.md: notes for whoever works on this next

- **Real people, with their OK.** Lois (Mai Kosai), her sister Racheal, their friend V, Oluwasegun and Mama Yan Bui are real and agreed to be in the game by name. No phone numbers, bank details or photos of them go in this public repo. The stall photos the layout came from stay out of the repo too.
- **The layout is from the photos; keep it.** The block house with the blue-trimmed roof; the zinc house is the open lean-to in front of it on three wooden posts, jutting toward the road. Racheal sits on a small stool inside it, frying at three stones. Lois stands outside beside the zinc house's end post, by the wall next to the black gate, at her table: green basket with holes for the kosai, pepper in a glass jar with a spoon, the red kunu warmer flask under the table. The long bench runs from the wall by the gate out toward the road. The crowd stands scattered around Lois's table, not in a line, all facing her. It is a university area: the customers are university students in everyday clothes (jeans, trousers, T-shirts, shirts, shorts), not ankara and no school backpacks, and about half are stylish ladies (braids, weaves, afro puffs, dresses, mini skirts, earrings, handbags). Mama Yan Bui supplies the awara: she walks in, puts it on Lois's table and tells her the total, which changes each time (AWARA_TOTAL in js/main.js; ₦3,000–₦7,500 pending the user's confirmation, they wrote 75,000). V's black sandal (too big for Lois, waiting for a buyer) sits by the post; tapping V brings up the sandal story.
- **Evening only**: the stall runs 5:30 to 9:30pm. The lantern comes on around 7pm.
- **Shared origin.** yilkash.github.io also serves Abide and the Mai Kosai order app, so localStorage keys start with `maiKosaiRush.` and the service worker is scoped to this folder. Never tell anyone to clear site data for yilkash.github.io.
- **No copyrighted music.** The groove in `js/audio.js` is original. Do not add recordings of real songs. It is rendered once into a buffer and looped: scheduling notes on a timer hung on phones busy drawing the scene.
- **Phones first.** Mid-range Android, often on small data plans: keep the first load small, pixel ratio capped at 1.5, one shadow-casting light.
- Prices for the gameplay: kosai ₦50 each, awara ₦50, puff-puff ₦100, kunu ₦300 a cup.
