# AGENTS.md: notes for whoever works on this next

- **Real people, with their OK.** Lois (Mai Kosai), her sister Racheal, their friend V, Oluwasegun and Mama Yan Bui are real and agreed to be in the game by name. No phone numbers, bank details or photos of them go in this public repo. The stall photos the layout came from stay out of the repo too.
- **The layout is from the photos; keep it.** The block house with the blue-trimmed roof; the zinc house is the open lean-to in front of it on three wooden posts, jutting toward the road. Racheal sits on a small stool inside it, frying at three stones. Lois stands outside beside the zinc house's end post, by the wall next to the black gate, at her table: green basket with holes for the kosai, pepper in a glass jar with a spoon, the red kunu warmer flask under the table. The long bench runs from the wall by the gate out toward the road. The crowd stands scattered around Lois's table, not in a line, all facing her. It is a university area: the customers are university students in everyday clothes (jeans, trousers, T-shirts, shirts, shorts), not ankara and no school backpacks, and about half are stylish ladies (braids, weaves, afro puffs, dresses, mini skirts, earrings, handbags). Mama Yan Bui supplies the awara: she drops it on Lois's table at opening and comes back at closing (9:30) to collect her money, which changes each time (AWARA_TOTAL in js/game.js: ₦3,000–₦7,500, confirmed by the user). V's black sandal (too big for Lois, waiting for a buyer) sits by the post; tapping V brings up the sandal story.
- **Evening only**: the stall runs 5:30 to 9:30pm. The lantern comes on around 7pm.
- **Shared origin.** yilkash.github.io also serves Abide and the Mai Kosai order app, so localStorage keys start with `maiKosaiRush.` and the service worker is scoped to this folder. Never tell anyone to clear site data for yilkash.github.io.
- **No copyrighted music.** The groove in `js/audio.js` is original. Do not add recordings of real songs. It is rendered once into a buffer and looped: scheduling notes on a timer hung on phones busy drawing the scene.
- **Phones first.** Mid-range Android, often on small data plans: keep the first load small, pixel ratio capped at 1.5, one shadow-casting light.
- **Gameplay (js/game.js).** Prices: kosai ₦50, awara ₦50, kunu ₦300 a cup (puff-puff is left out on purpose). Mama Yan Bui's awara costs ₦40 a piece to Lois. One game minute per real second. Modals pause the clock. The kiosk tin is saved as `maiKosaiRush.tin` and the evening reached as `maiKosaiRush.evening`. Each evening has a sales target (EVENINGS in game.js); hitting it puts the profit in the tin and opens the next evening, which adds one new thing (FEATURES: transfers, NEPA, arguments, Oluwasegun's rush, V's sandal).
- **Keep the screen clear.** In-game choices go in the bottom bar (`ask()` in game.js), never a pop-up over the stall; only the start, pause and end-of-night screens are centred. Speech bubbles are short and at most two at once; a sale shows a small "+₦" instead of a thank-you.
- **Lois moves like she does at the stall:** bends to the red kunu flask under the table for kunu, reaches into the basket for kosai, hands orders over, and rests with a hand on her hip when idle.
- **Two views, both kept (the user loves both).** Table view (default) is over Lois's shoulder, across her table at the crowd. Stall view is the whole place with name tags over the regulars. The button switches, and the game cuts to Stall view on events (`focus()` from game.js). The table camera for sideways matches the mock the user approved.
- **Serving is a written card, not tapping 3D things.** The user couldn't make a sale by tapping the basket and flask, and found picture bubbles unclear. Orders are in words ("4 kosai, pepper"); tap the customer or their bubble and a card at the bottom has + buttons and Pack & give. The first customer of evening 1 comes alone and the game pulses the next thing to tap. Racheal fries by herself (the corner pan window confused the user).
- **The opening (cast.js `opening`).** They don't live at the house with the gate: Racheal and Lois walk up from the road with loads on their heads. Lois sets her tray on the table; Racheal puts her basin down, goes into the house through the door and brings the pan out; Lois goes off for water and comes back with a bucket on her head; Racheal sits and lights the fire. Skip jumps to the end. Then Mama Yan Bui brings the awara and tells Lois the price out loud (₦3,000–7,500) before she leaves.
- **Oluwasegun must be noticeable**: his green "post on WhatsApp" button is there from evening 1, he introduces himself after the first sale, and Stall view names him.
- **Lois wears a top and jeans, never a wrapper** ("she is my beautiful badie"). The bench friend's name is spelled **Oluwasegun**.
